The whiteboard

My research

Four interests, one question: what happens to people when software and AI organize their work, and what should we build once we know?

I study it with mixed methods, then write the tools the findings call for.

01

AI and the future of work

How automation, generative AI and algorithmic management reshape jobs, skills and inequality, starting with the workers who feel it first. Published in Sociology Lens, with a second study at the revise-and-resubmit stage.

02

Human–computer interaction

How students and workers actually use AI tools. I analyze thousands of student conversations with an AI tutor chatbot and build learning tools for Gravity Spy, the citizen-science project that supports LIGO.

03

Digital labor and online freelancers

Platform work and the algorithms that manage it. My master's thesis follows Bangladeshi freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr through 24 in-depth interviews — work I did myself first, with 100+ client projects.

04

ICT for development

How information systems, platforms and mobile internet open, or close, economic opportunity in the Global South, and how AI is reshaping that path for workers in countries like Bangladesh.
