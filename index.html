What I'm working on

Research interests

Four connected interests, one question: what happens to people when software and AI organize their work — and what should we build once we know? I study it with mixed methods, then write the tools the findings call for.

01 · Interest

AI and the future of work

How automation, generative AI and algorithmic management reshape jobs, skills and inequality — starting with the workers who feel it first. Published in Sociology Lens, with a second study at revise-and-resubmit.

02 · Interest

Human–computer interaction

How students and workers actually use AI agents and tools. I analyze thousands of student conversations with an AI tutor chatbot (AI Programming Research Group) and build learning tools for Gravity Spy, the citizen-science project behind LIGO.

03 · Interest

Digital labor & online freelancers

Platform and gig work and the algorithms that manage it. My master's thesis follows Bangladeshi freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr through 24 in-depth interviews — work I did myself first, with 100+ client projects.

04 · Interest

ICT for development (ICT4D)

How information systems, platforms and mobile internet open — or close — economic opportunity in the Global South, and how AI is reshaping that path for workers in countries like Bangladesh.
