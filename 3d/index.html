01 / Research interests

Four interests, one question

What happens to people when software and AI organize their work — and what should we build once we know?

01

AI and the future of work

How automation, generative AI and algorithmic management reshape jobs, skills and inequality, starting with the workers who feel it first. Published in Sociology Lens.

02

Human–computer interaction

How students and workers actually use AI tools: thousands of conversations with an AI tutor chatbot, and learning tools for Gravity Spy, which supports LIGO.

03

Digital labor and online freelancers

Platform work and the algorithms that manage it. My thesis follows Bangladeshi freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr through 24 in-depth interviews.

04

ICT for development

How platforms and mobile internet open, or close, economic opportunity in the Global South, and how AI is reshaping that path.
