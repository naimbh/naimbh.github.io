Custom Laravel and Vue.js web applications, admin dashboards, real-time features, API integrations (Twilio, WhatsApp, Meta, REST), converting existing sites to Laravel, and bug fixes or new features on existing PHP projects.

How do I hire you?

Send a short brief through Upwork, Fiverr or email at contact@naimbh.com. You'll get questions and a fixed-price or milestone quote, usually within a day.

Can you work on my existing Laravel or Vue codebase?

Yes. Many past projects were customizations of existing Laravel applications — payroll systems, Laravel 8 + Vue 2 apps and Twilio-based platforms — fixing bugs and shipping new features without rewriting what works.

Which time zone do you work in?

I'm based in Syracuse, New York (US Eastern Time) and have worked remotely with clients around the world.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a handover with documentation, and ongoing maintenance or feature work can continue on an hourly or milestone basis.

Are you still freelancing while doing a PhD?

Yes, on a limited number of well-scoped projects. I build research software at Syracuse University every week, so my engineering stays current. Curious about the research? Visit my academic site.
